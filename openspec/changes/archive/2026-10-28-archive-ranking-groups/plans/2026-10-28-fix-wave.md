# Archive ranking groups: fix wave of the final review (task group 6) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Close the findings of the final review: the default `archive_at` follows later contests (owner's decision), the fill is fully inside its error handling, and Regenerate cannot reset a group that becomes archived while it waits.

**Architecture:** Two edits in `cms/service/ProxyService.py` (`_fill_archive_defaults`, `_regenerate_ranking_sync`) and their tests in `cmstestsuite/unit_tests/service/proxyservice_groups_test.py`.

**Spec:** `specs/ranking-group-archiving/spec.md`: requirement "archive_at gets a visible default" (revised: three Scenarios changed or added: "A past archive_at is kept", "A contest added later postpones a future archive_at", "A later archive_at is not moved earlier") and "Regenerate does nothing for an archived group"; `design.md` decisions 6 and 8 and the risk "A stale default"; tasks 6.1 and 6.2 of `tasks.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Postpone only: when `archive_at` is in the future and the computed default is strictly later, set it to the default; never move it earlier; never touch an `archive_at <= now`; fill an empty one only if the default is strictly in the future (unchanged).
- The whole fill (reads and the UPDATE that autoflush emits) is inside the error handling: a database error is logged as one WARNING, rolled back, and the sweep goes on (the session must not leak a `PendingRollbackError`).
- Regenerate: a group that is archived at ANY point between the start of the call and the enqueue of its operations gets no RESET, no data and no final-visibility mark left set.
- Tick 6.1 and 6.2 in `tasks.md` in their commits. All code, comments and commit messages in English; Conventional Commits with the `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` trailer.

## Review Focus

- Two groups, the first UPDATE fails at the autoflush of the second group's SELECT: one WARNING, rollback, the sweep still enqueues visibility and data, the next sweep stores both defaults. Task 1.
- A group with `archive_at` = default exactly: not changed (not strictly later). Task 1.
- Many groups: one aggregate query per candidate group per sweep, no N+1 on contests. Task 1.
- The race of Task 2 simulated deterministically by making `make_datetime` return an open time for the first call and an archived time for the following ones (no threads in the test).
